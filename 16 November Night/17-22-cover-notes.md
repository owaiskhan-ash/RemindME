# Cover notes: 17-22

The six JPGs are single-pass AI generations, not two-layer composites. Each image was generated in one unified pass from the supplied cover photo as reference, with the book, surface, lighting, shadow, floating elements and blurred background rendered together as one continuous image. The cover artwork, printed lettering and layout were instructed to be preserved exactly as photographed rather than redrawn or redesigned. Each portrait export is 928 x 1,152 pixels (4:5) at native generation size; the images were not upscaled to 6,400 x 8,000 and are not native 8K. Themes are inferred from the covers.

Notes on corrections in this batch: the 3-D Beasts source photo was rotated 90 degrees on upload; the book's true orientation is portrait (orange banner on top), and the reference was read and reproduced in that upright orientation. "What a Wonderful World" carried a red retail price sticker in the photo; the sticker was omitted from the generated marketing image because it is not part of the cover artwork. Two covers needed regeneration: Puppy Love came out at a three-quarter angle at first and was regenerated dead front-facing, and What a Wonderful World kept sinking into foreground meadow grass that hid the book's bottom edge in two attempts before being regenerated on a flat wooden garden bench, leaving the complete cover rectangle, bottom edge included, fully visible.
