# Cover notes: 06–11

The six JPGs use the supplied photographs of the physical covers, rotated upright where necessary and perspective-corrected before being composited into AI-generated marketing scenes. The original artwork, printed lettering, stickers, sound panel, tabs, cutouts and wear have not been redrawn. Each portrait export is 6,400 × 8,000 pixels (4:5); the generated backgrounds were enlarged from 928 × 1,152 pixels and are not native 8K. Themes are inferred from the covers.
