The five JPGs are single-pass AI generations, not two-layer composites. Each image was generated in one unified pass from the supplied cover photo as reference, with the book, surface, lighting, shadow, floating elements and blurred background rendered together as one continuous image. The cover artwork, printed lettering and layout were instructed to be preserved exactly as photographed rather than redrawn or redesigned. Each portrait export is 928 x 1,152 pixels (4:5) at native generation size; the images were not upscaled to 6,400 x 8,000 and are not native 8K. Themes are inferred from the covers.
