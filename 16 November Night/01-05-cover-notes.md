# Cover notes: 01–05

The five JPGs use the actual photographed cover faces, rotated upright where necessary and perspective-corrected before being composited into AI-generated marketing scenes. Printed lettering, illustrations, stickers, barcodes, and existing wear come from the supplied photos rather than being redrawn. Each portrait export is 6,400 × 8,000 pixels (4:5); the generated backgrounds were enlarged from 928 × 1,152 pixels and are not native 8K. Themes are inferred from the covers.
